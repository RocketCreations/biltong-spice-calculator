      Order of work

        Salt is the one rate worth tuning to taste — 18 g/kg is the classic droëwors cure, and most makers settle somewhere between 15 and 21. Below about 15 g/kg you are relying on the vinegar and the drying alone, so keep the airflow good and the batch small. Every other quantity stays fixed per kilogram. Spoon equivalents are approximate — spice density varies with grind and how hard you pack the spoon, so weigh whenever you can. Coarse sea salt only; iodised salt will taint the flavour.
